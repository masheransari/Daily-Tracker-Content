Daily Tracker – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · App: Daily Tracker (app.dotinfiny.dailytracker)

This policy explains what Daily Tracker stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have. Daily Tracker is developed by Muhammad Asher Ansari. It has no user accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics of its own and no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

1. Information you enter

The app stores what you type: transactions (amount, currency, type, category, date and note), loans and repayments (the name of the other person, due dates and, if an earlier version saved one, a contact detail), monthly budgets, and your settings (default currency, theme and date format).

This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device (a local database and a settings file). The developer does not run a server for the app and does not receive your records. The app does not encrypt this storage itself; it relies on Android's app sandbox and your device's own protections, such as its screen lock.

If you record another person's name or contact detail, please only record what you are entitled to keep.

2. Android backup and device transfer

The app allows Android backup and does not exclude any of its data. This means your records and settings can be copied:

  • To your Google account by Android Auto Backup, if backup is turned on in your device settings. Google stores the backup in a private area of your Google Drive. On Android 9 and later with a screen lock, Google states these backups are end-to-end encrypted with your PIN, pattern or password.
  • To a new phone during a device-to-device transfer.

Backups are made and kept by Android and Google, not by the developer. You can turn backup off in your device settings. The app has no export or import feature of its own.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). This SDK is separate from the app's own storage. It never receives your transactions, loans or budgets, but it does collect and send data to Google automatically, including:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and app identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches and ad taps);
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google states this data is encrypted in transit and used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads may be personalised. Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) sends a crash report so the developer can fix the problem. A report contains the technical error and the app's state at the time, basic device information (such as model, Android version and free memory) and random installation identifiers used to count how many people a crash affects. It does not intentionally include your transactions, loans, budgets or notes. Google states this data is encrypted in transit. Crash reports are not used for advertising.

5. Your choices about ads

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's Google or privacy settings. Using the app without a network connection stops ads from loading.

6. Deleting records and uninstalling

  • Deleting a record removes it from the app's database. Deleting a loan also deletes its recorded repayments. The app does not securely overwrite storage.
  • If the app was updated from an older version, a copy of your older records may be kept inside the app's database as an upgrade safety copy. Deleting a record in the app does not remove that copy.
  • Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes all of the app's data from the device, including those copies.
  • A Google backup made before you deleted something may still contain it, and may be restored if you reinstall the app. Backups are controlled by your Google account and device settings, not by the app.
  • Data already sent to Google by the advertising SDK is handled under Google's policies.

7. Contacting support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and whatever you write. The support email the app prepares includes only the app version and Android version; it never attaches your records, contacts or device identifiers. Please don't send financial records. Support emails are used only to answer your request.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults managing their own finances and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
