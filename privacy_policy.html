Privacy Policy

App: Daily Tracker
Last updated: October 13, 2026

Daily Tracker is developed by Muhammad Asher Ansari. This policy explains how information is handled by the Android app app.dotinfiny.dailytracker.

Summary: Your finance records are stored locally without an app account or developer-operated sync service. Android backup and device transfer may copy app data off the device. Advertising services process technical data separately.

1. Records and preferences

The app stores information you enter: income, expenses, amounts, currencies, categories, dates, notes, budgets, loans and repayments. Loan records can include another person's name and optional contact details. It also stores preferences such as currency, theme and date format. Enter only information you are entitled to record.

2. Purpose and local storage

These records are used to display transaction history, recorded balances, budgets, charts and loan progress. They are stored in the app's local database and preference storage. Daily Tracker does not provide bank connections, account registration or developer-operated cloud synchronization. The developer does not receive your financial records through normal finance tracking.

3. Android backup and device transfer

Android backup and device transfer can include the app database and settings, depending on device capabilities, your settings and the backup provider. This may include retained copies of older database tables created during upgrades. Backup copies can therefore contain earlier financial records. They are managed by the platform or backup provider, rather than a Daily Tracker server.

4. Advertising and technical data

Daily Tracker integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google documents collection and sharing of IP addresses, product interactions, diagnostic information and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. Identifier availability and advertising behavior depend on device settings, SDK configuration and applicable consent requirements. Your manually entered finance records are not intentionally supplied to AdMob.

Advertising-related processing is separate from your local finance records. Non-personalized advertising does not necessarily eliminate technical data processing. Review Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures for details.

5. Network access and choices

Finance tracking works offline. Network access supports advertising; opening external links or sending support email uses the selected external service. Device advertising settings may let you reset or delete the advertising identifier or adjust advertising preferences. Where the app presents advertising consent or privacy controls, you can use those controls to review your choices. Availability depends on the installed version and region.

6. Support and sharing

If you email support, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send, including any app or Android version information included in your message. This information is used to respond and investigate the issue. Avoid sending sensitive financial records unless necessary. Support messages are retained only as reasonably needed for support, dispute resolution or legal obligations. Your email provider also processes the message. Sharing the app uses your selected sharing service; it does not automatically share finance records.

7. Retention and deletion

Active records remain until you delete them or remove local app data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling generally removes the local app data but does not necessarily remove platform backups, historical migration copies, advertising-service data or previously sent support messages. Deleting an active record does not necessarily delete a retained historical table copy or existing backup. Restoring a backup may restore earlier records. Manage backups through your device or backup-provider settings. There is no developer-operated finance account to delete and no in-app export/import service in the documented version.

8. Security

Local storage is subject to Android's app isolation and your device's security. Do not assume a separate app-level encryption layer or guaranteed recovery. No device, backup or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children

Daily Tracker is a personal finance utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly solicit children's personal information through support. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support so we can review and address it.

10. Third parties and international processing

Google, backup providers, email providers and services you open have their own privacy practices. Their processing may occur outside your country. The developer does not sell your manually entered financial records.

11. Requests and policy changes

Contact us to ask about information you have provided directly to support or to request its deletion, subject to applicable retention obligations. We cannot directly retrieve or delete finance records stored only on your device or copies controlled by your backup provider. This policy may change as the app changes; revisions will be posted with an updated date.
